# Code of conduct

This project should be a useful, careful place for researchers, learners,
clinicians, engineers, and annotators.

## Expected behaviour

- Discuss evidence and implementation without attacking the person.
- Be specific about uncertainty, limitations, and conflicting sources.
- Respect different levels of technical and clinical experience.
- Protect private, sensitive, and unpublished research information.
- Credit the people, software, datasets, and prior work that made a
  contribution possible.
- Accept a correction when stronger evidence changes the record.

Harassment, discriminatory language, threats, deliberate misrepresentation,
unwanted disclosure of personal information, and repeated bad-faith disruption
are not accepted.

## Reporting

For an ordinary disagreement about code or evidence, use the relevant issue
template. For conduct concerns that should not be public, contact the maintainer
through the private contact route on
[omarchoudhry.co.uk](https://omarchoudhry.co.uk). Reports will be reviewed
privately and handled in proportion to the risk.

Maintainers may edit, hide, reject, or remove contributions and participation
that breach this policy. They will avoid public disclosure of a reporter's
identity unless required for safety or law.
