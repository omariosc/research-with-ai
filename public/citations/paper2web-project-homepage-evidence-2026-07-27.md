## Counts used

Paper2Web reports a corpus of papers from selected major AI conferences
published between 2020 and 2025:

| Website status | Papers | Share |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| Verified human-created project homepage | 10,716 | 11.1% |
| No project homepage identified | 85,843 | 88.9% |
| Total | 96,559 | 100.0% |

The displayed percentages are derived from the published counts:

```text
10,716 / 96,559 × 100 = 11.0976% → 11.1%
85,843 / 96,559 × 100 = 88.9024% → 88.9%
```

The paper also reports manually checking a sample of 2,000 homepages when
studying site characteristics. The tutorial does not publish the preprint's
four-way characteristic percentages because their displayed values sum to
101.0%, while the accepted ACL text does not provide the corresponding
percentages for clarification.

## Measurement boundary

The authors searched for relevant website links in papers and associated code
repositories, then resolved ambiguous candidates and retained human-created
project homepages. Therefore:

- "No homepage identified" does not prove that no site existed.
- An unlinked site or a site published after collection may have been missed.
- The result describes discoverability in this selected AI-conference corpus,
  not all scientific publishing, healthcare research, or current papers.
- The count does not show that creating a website causes more citations,
  downloads, reuse, or scientific impact.
- A project homepage is not automatically interactive, accessible, accurate,
  maintained, or useful.

## Graphic and reuse decision

The tutorial renders an original HTML and CSS stacked bar from the two counts.
Its accessible name states the denominator, counts, and percentages. A text
legend and this record provide a non-visual route to the same information.

No figure, screenshot, code, or website asset from Paper2Web, Paper2All, or a
third-party project homepage is copied. Paper2All is linked only as a design
reference because no top-level licence file was detected during the repository
review.

## Claim used in the tutorial

In the 96,559-paper Paper2Web corpus from selected major AI conferences,
10,716 papers, or 11.1%, had a verified project homepage. The statistic shows
that project websites are established but not routine in that sample. It is a
motivation to make research easier to discover and explore, not evidence that a
website changes research impact.
