# Research with AI v1.4.0

Released 27 July 2026.

Version 1.4.0 brings all four tutorials into one reviewed release.

## What changed

- Released **Run an AI in Healthcare Conference** with a first-hand Leeds case,
  programme-change record, responsibility map, denominator-aware evaluation,
  media boundaries, and local conference planner.
- Rebuilt the annotation demonstration around the public frame-annotator and
  surgical-annotator workflows, with an exact first-three frame allowlist and
  three disclosed LASK examples.
- Added the annotation research-outcome chronology from the Hamlyn group
  project and MIUA work to LASK and BTPN.
- Added the Paper2Web project-homepage prevalence panel with an original chart
  and explicit measurement limits.
- Made AI paper review a recurring, human-owned part of the research lifecycle.
- Refreshed version metadata, citations, screenshots, release evidence, and the
  annotation specification schema.

## Evidence and limitations

The [release evidence record](../../public/audits/platform-release-v1.4.0-2026-07-27.md)
documents sources, rights decisions, privacy boundaries, checks, and remaining
human review. In particular:

- identifiable conference workbooks remain outside the public repository;
- conference photographs retain project-specific permission rather than a
  general reuse licence;
- the frame demonstration publishes only its approved first three samples;
- the surgical demonstration publishes exactly three attributed LASK examples;
  and
- learner-pilot, screen-reader, video, and final submission work remain named
  human tasks rather than claimed completed checks.
